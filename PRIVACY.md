## Information the app stores

Your progress (best score, shards, unlocked skins, settings, streak) is stored only on your device.
We don't run servers for the app and don't receive this data. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

## Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process
your device's advertising ID, IP address (used for approximate location), information about how you
interact with ads, and diagnostic data. In regions where consent is required (for example the EEA and
UK), the app asks for your consent before loading personalized ads. You can reset or delete your
advertising ID in your device's settings.

Learn how Google uses this information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

## Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play. We don't receive your payment details. Google Play
tells the app which items you own so they can be unlocked and restored.

## Children

The app isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.
