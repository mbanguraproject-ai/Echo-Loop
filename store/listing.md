## Full description
Your best run never leaves. It races you.

Echo Loop is a neon synthwave runner. Swipe across three lanes of glowing highway to dodge walls, jump barriers, slide under laser gates and slip past data trains. The faster you go, the tighter it gets. Every best run you set becomes your echo: a pink copy of you that replays your exact moves, lane for lane, on your next run. Catch it. Pass it. Then set a new one.

HOW TO PLAY
• Swipe left or right to switch lanes
• Swipe up to jump barriers
• Swipe down to slide under lasers (swipe down in mid-air to drop fast)
• Grab shards and power-ups along the way
• Crash? Rewind time a few seconds and keep your run

POWER-UPS
• Magnet: pulls in every shard nearby
• Shield: takes one hit for you
• Double Shards: twice the haul
• Hyperboost: fly above the highway at full speed

THREE ZONES
• Neon Grid, Solar Drift and Void Rift, a new look every 1,000 m
• The road speeds up the longer you survive

ALWAYS SOMETHING TO CHASE
• Race your echo in Endless, and today's best in the Daily Run
• Daily Run: the same track all day with a new twist (Laser Storm, Rail Day, Low Gravity, Blackout…)
• Missions that raise your score multiplier permanently, up to ×30
• Upgrades that make every power-up last longer, plus a head start
• Daily streak rewards
• Neon riders to unlock: Ember, Toxic, Glacier, Sakura, Aurum, Spectrum

One thumb, instant restarts, and a soundtrack that speeds up with you.

Can you outrun your echo?
